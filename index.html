A Place I Call Home

South Africa is a vibrant, multiethnic constitutional democracy located at the southern tip of the African continent, bordered by the Atlantic and Indian Oceans. Known as the "rainbow nation" for its rich cultural and linguistic diversity-featuring 11 official languages—the country has three capital cities: Pretoria (administrative), Cape Town (legislative), and Bloemfontein (judicial). Following the end of the apartheid regime and the historic 1994 democratic election of Nelson Mandela, South Africa established itself as a major economy on the continent, renowned for its diverse landscapes, wildlife, and complex history.
